\section{Character Theory}

  \begin{definition}[Character]
    \label{def:rep-character}
    The \textbf{character} of a representation $(V, \rho)$ is the function $\chi_V: G \to \mathbb{C}$ given by 
    \begin{equation}
      \chi_V(g) \coloneqq \Tr\big( \rho(g) \big)
    \end{equation}
  \end{definition}

  Characters are constant on conjugacy classes, since the trace is invariant under conjugation. TBD: orthogonality relations, character tables.

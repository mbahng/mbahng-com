C4. Statistics/Linear_Regression does not survive a cold build.
    From a clean tree (`latexmk -C` first) it fails with
    "! Package natbib Error: Bibliography not compatible with author-year
    citations", leaving 39 references unresolved on that pass. Running pdflatex a
    few more times converges it to a correct PDF with zero errors, which is why a
    warm rebuild looks fine -- but a fresh checkout will hit the error.
    Cause: natbib is resolving citations in author-year mode while
    \bibliographystyle{alpha} (paper.tex:291) emits alpha labels
    (\bibitem[GKKW02]{...}), which natbib cannot parse as author-year. The document
    only ever uses plain \cite, so the one-line fix is
    \usepackage[numbers]{natbib}; switching to plainnat or abbrvnat would also work.
    This predates my changes (confirmed against a pristine checkout of HEAD).

D8. "properly discontinuous", and an action attributed to a product.
    Math/Algebraic_Topology/paper.tex:356
    "$G \times X$ is \textbf{proper discontinuous} if for all $x \in X$ ..." -- the
    standard term is "properly discontinuous", and it is the *action* of $G$ on $X$
    that has the property, not the product $G \times X$.
